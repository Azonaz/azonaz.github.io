# Map My Days

**Plan places. Build your trip.** 

Map My Days is a simple travel planner for people who prefer to organize their own trips.

Save the places you want to visit, organize them by day, set priorities, and keep backup options for changes in weather or schedule.

## Turn Places Into A Trip Plan

A trip often starts with a long list of places.

Map My Days helps you turn that list into a clear day-by-day plan.

Add the places that interest you, keep some unplanned for later, and assign others to specific days when your route starts to take shape.

## See The Whole Trip On The Map

View all your saved places together and filter them by day.

The map makes it easier to see which places are close to each other, which ones fit better on another day, and where your accommodation is in relation to your plans.

You can also add new places directly from the map.

## Keep Your Plan Flexible

Travel plans change.

Mark places as must-see, nice-to-have, or backup options. Add weather preferences and keep indoor alternatives ready for rainy days.

If something changes, move places between days instead of rebuilding the whole trip.

## Add Your Accommodation

Add one place to stay or several.

Each accommodation has its own dates and location, so it appears on the map for the relevant part of your trip.

This works for both a short city break with one hotel and a longer trip with several stops.

## Simple By Design

Map My Days focuses on planning places by day.

No complex booking system.  
No budget management.  
No automatic trip generation.

Just your places, your days, and your plan.

## FAQ

### Can I keep places without assigning them to a day?

Yes. Places can stay unplanned until you decide where they fit.

### Can I plan for bad weather?

Yes. You can add weather preferences and keep backup places for days when your original plan no longer works.

### Can I see my places on a map?

Yes. You can see all trip places on the map and filter them by day.

### Can I choose which navigation app opens my places?

Yes. In Settings, you can choose your preferred external navigation app. Links to places will open in the app you select.

### Does Map My Days require a subscription?

No. Map My Days does not use a recurring subscription.

### Is Map My Days available for iPhone and iPad?

Yes.
